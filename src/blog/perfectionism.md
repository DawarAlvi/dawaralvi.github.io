It is quite often when I sit down to write, even before I type a single character, doubt creeps into my mind. If what I write is not perfect in every way is it even worth writing? It sounds absurd when I say it like that. But this is the best I can describe the feeling, whenever I decide not just to write, but to undertake any creative task that I know will be viewed by someone other than myself.

The titular aphorism never sat well with me. I always used to think how can perfect be a bad aim to have. Isn't perfect just 'perfect', the ultimate good, the highest level of any attainable art form must _be_ perfection. Turns out I didn't understand the phrase at all. While the pursuit for perfection is a noble cause to have, the path that leads to this destination is littered with mistakes, mishaps and imperfect abandoned projects. But they were necessary, they were the stepping stones that one must cross to get closer to the ever so coveted perfection.
Attaining perfection is a impossibility ofcourse. We humans by our very nature are imperfect beings. And the things we make can never be 100% perfect in every way. This is always room for improvement.

I don't fear the man who has practiced a thousand punches. But I fear the man who a practiced a punch a thousand times.

Mistakes are your best teacher.

Damned is the artist who is so crippled by his fear of making a wrong mark that he never lifts his brush, never marks the canvas with a single stroke, and worst of all, never learns. 

Self doubt.
Sometimes you are your own worst critic.

How will what i write be perceived by other people. I create imaginary critics that I want to try to impress. And they never are impressed. 

I have decided to focus on quantity rather than quality for this blog. That isn't to say I'll be posting low effort slop but I'll try not to hold myself to an unimaginably high standard that I know from that get go, I won't be able to meet. Otherwise I would be setting myself up for failure.
